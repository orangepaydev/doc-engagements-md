What transfers

Borrow China's sequence, not its enforcement.

What transfers well

  • The order: process and data before platforms and AI
  • "One visit" targets and life-event bundles, which are commitments, not mandates
  • Rule-based approval with a manual fallback
  • Writing reforms into state rules, so they outlast leadership changes

Johor's realities

  • Federal, state and 16 councils agree by consensus, not by mandate
  • The Data Sharing Act 2025 covers federal agencies only, not the state
  • Two-speed citizens: physical counters must stay open
  • Little in-house technical capacity in the district councils
Don't import: blacklisting or pervasive surveillance. Both would cost the data lake public trust.

The critical path

The critical path is a state data-sharing framework.

The data lakeStorage: data held in one place
+ A state data-sharing frameworkA state directive or enactment, a data catalogue, and standard agreements between state, councils and federal bodies
Shared, trusted dataThe base for quick wins, automation and new services

Why Johor needs its own

The Data Sharing Act 2025 covers federal public-sector bodies only. Any data flow between state agencies, the 16 councils and federal bodies needs its own legal basis.

Without it, the data lake is storage. With it, the data lake is the foundation for everything else.

How to deliver

Six principles for how to deliver it.

1

The state owns; an operator runs

Government keeps the data, the rules and the outcomes. A dedicated operator builds and runs the platform.

2

Shared services for councils

District councils use shared services, rather than each buying its own package.

3

Fund running costs, not just the build

The biggest failures were systems nobody was paid to run after launch.

4

Measure use, not launches

Usage and outcome targets set from day one, and published.

5

Write reforms into state rules

So that the reforms outlast changes in leadership.

6

Keep the right to change operators

Choose operators competitively, with term limits and an exit clause, and keep the data with the state.

From the findings

Three findings shape how this should be delivered.

1

The order matters

Process and data foundations come before platforms and AI. Skipping ahead is how projects go idle.

2

The critical path is governance

A state data-sharing framework, and agreement across 16 councils. Neither of these is a software problem.

3

Delivery must expect change

With consensus across councils and new priorities from the data-centre boom and the SEZ, direction will shift.

So the delivery approach must keep the state in control, and make changing direction cheap.

The usual risk

The usual risk: the definition is locked in before anyone has learned.

Specifya long specification, written up front
Tenderand contract against it
Buildfor months
The need has movedby go-live
Change costsa variation order, or a rewrite

What China's failures show

Turnkey smart-city packages went idle within months when priorities moved or data was not shared. Technology placed on top of unchanged processes delivered little.

Why it matters more in Johor

Sixteen councils deciding by consensus, plus fast-moving demands from the data-centre boom and the SEZ, mean the definition will change during delivery.

Illustrative example

When a renewal rule changes, Johor doesn't start again.

Every step uses what works today. It shows how the approach works; it is not a customer case study.

1
Policy
The state adds a check before a business licence can be renewed automatically.
2
Officer
An officer updates the rule in Hyalo's visual rule view, with no new tender.
3
Hyalo
Automated checks run, and Hyalo shows the result.
4
Service owner
The accountable owner approves. The approval is recorded.
5
Hyalo Runtime
The new version runs. The previous version stays on record.
The record shows what changed, who approved it, and which version each service runs.

Our starting position

Our starting position on the board's first questions.

Hosting and data residency

Proposed

Hyalo Runtime and your source code repository run in your environment, in-country: your own cloud or on-premise.

AI model

Proposed

Your choice of model, including self-hosted or in-country compute, such as through MDEC's planned National AI Compute Exchange.

Security

Proposed

Align to the government's security framework, with independent security testing before any live use.

Procurement

Proposed

Start small, at a scope that fits simple procurement routes.

Skills and support

Proposed

We work alongside state and council teams at the start, and train officers to review and approve changes.

Continuity and exit

Proposed

Designs and their full history stay in your repository. We propose source-code escrow for Hyalo Runtime.

These are proposals, not terms. We'd rather refine them with you than assume them.
